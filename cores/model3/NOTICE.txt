RetroPixels' Sega Model 3 emulator (cores/model3) - third-party parts
=====================================================================

Most of this package was written for RetroPixels from the hardware's
behaviour, with Supermodel (https://github.com/trzy/Supermodel, the Sega
Model 3 emulator by The Supermodel Team) as the reference its pictures and
sound were compared against. These parts follow other projects' code
closely and keep those projects' licences:

- scsp.go: the Yamaha SCSP sound chips. A Go port of Supermodel's SCSP
  emulation (Src/Sound/SCSP.cpp, SCSPDSP.cpp, SCSPLFO.cpp; written by ElSemi,
  with fixes from MAME by R. Belmont and others), Copyright 2003-2026 The
  Supermodel Team. GPL-3.0-or-later: see LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt.
- dsb.go: the MPEG music board (DSB2). Its command handling, volume and
  stereo modes follow Supermodel's Src/Model3/DSB.cpp. GPL-3.0-or-later:
  see LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt.
- crypto.go, crypto_tables.go: the Sega 315-5881 decryption, from MAME's
  sega315_5881 emulation (Andreas Naive, Olivier Galibert, David Haywood),
  BSD-3-Clause (text below).
- mpeg.go: the MPEG Layer I/II decoder, a port of minimp3 by Lion
  (lieff), released into the public domain (CC0).

Because scsp.go and dsb.go are GPL-3.0-or-later, the RetroPixels programs
that include this emulator (RetroPixels.exe, RetroPixels Native and
RetroPixels for Android) are distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later as a whole;
their full source is attached to each release.
