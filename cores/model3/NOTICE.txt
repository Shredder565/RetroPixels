RetroPixels' Sega Model 3 emulator (cores/model3) - third-party parts
=====================================================================

This package was written for RetroPixels from the hardware's behaviour,
with Supermodel (https://github.com/trzy/Supermodel, the Sega Model 3
emulator by The Supermodel Team) as the reference its pictures and sound
were compared against. Since RetroPixels 2.46.0 the sound chips (scsp.go),
the music board (dsb.go) and the MPEG decoder (mpeg.go) are RetroPixels'
own code too; they no longer contain Supermodel's or minimp3's code.

One part comes from MAME and keeps MAME's licence for it:

- crypto.go, crypto_tables.go: the Sega 315-5881 decryption, from MAME's
  sega315_5881 emulation (Andreas Naive, Olivier Galibert, David Haywood),
  BSD-3-Clause (text below).
