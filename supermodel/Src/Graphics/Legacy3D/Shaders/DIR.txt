Original code for RetroPixels (Supermodel). Not derived from Supermodel.

Reference GLSL files. The renderers do not read this folder: the programs
they use are built in (Legacy3D/Shaders3D.h, Graphics/Shaders2D.h). Vertex,
Fragment and Fragment_MultiSheet are copies of the built-in Legacy3D
programs; Fragment_NoSpotlight and Fragment_Flat are simpler variants. Any
of them can be loaded with the VertexShader / FragmentShader settings.
