RetroPixels change log
======================

Every version of RetroPixels, newest first. The version you have shows next
to the RetroPixels name in the app and in VERSION.txt.

RetroPixels 2.9.0  (2026-10-15)
-------------------------------
- New: RetroPixels-Native.exe, the first test build of RetroPixels rebuilt as
  a native Windows program with no web browser inside, so OBS Studio's Window
  Capture records it with any capture method. It plays NES and Atari 2600
  games with the same emulation as RetroPixels.exe (checked frame by frame
  and sample by sample against it), Xbox controllers, save states, battery
  saves and game-only recording. The other systems follow in later test
  builds; RetroPixels.exe is unchanged and still plays everything.

RetroPixels 2.8.0  (2026-10-15)
-------------------------------
- New: Archipelago (archipelago.gg) multiworld randomizers. Open an
  Archipelago patch file (.apsm, .aplttp, .apred, .apemerald and others) like
  a game; RetroPixels asks once for the original ROM, checks it, keeps it for
  that game and makes the randomized game, with its own saves. Patched ROMs
  made with the Archipelago Launcher open too.
- The Archipelago SNI Client (Super Nintendo) and BizHawk Client (Game Boy /
  Color, Game Boy Advance, NES, Nintendo 64) connect to RetroPixels directly;
  SNI, BizHawk and Lua scripts aren't needed. An "Archipelago" button under
  the game shows whether the client is connected and how to connect it.
  RetroPixels only answers programs on this computer.

RetroPixels 2.7.1  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- RetroPixels has its own icon: the warrior picture now shows on the
  desktop, in Explorer, on the taskbar and on the window.
- 4-player arcade games (TMNT, Turtles in Time, The Simpsons) show "Choose
  your player" buttons above the game: 1 Leonardo, 2 Michelangelo,
  3 Donatello, 4 Raphael, or 1 Marge, 2 Homer, 3 Bart, 4 Lisa. Your keyboard
  and first controller play as the one you pick; it's remembered per game.
- The program's Properties in Windows show its name and version.

RetroPixels 2.7.0  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- Arcade: TMNT, Turtles in Time (TMNT2) and The Simpsons were each played
  start to finish in MAME and in RetroPixels and compared frame by frame and
  sound by sound. The picture now matches MAME pixel for pixel in almost
  every frame checked, and the sound balance matches. RetroPixels' code is
  still all its own: MAME was only watched and listened to.
- New: 4-player arcade games (and Nintendo 64 and Genesis, which have four
  controller ports) have a "Player 1" button above the game.
  Click it to choose which player (1 to 4) your keyboard and first
  controller play as. The choice is remembered for each game.
- TMNT: a stray dark mark on the title screen is gone; flip-screen mode now
  flips the sprites too; drum and effect volumes were swapped between the
  two sample channels; speech is no longer cut off by the next line; music
  noise (cymbals, hits) was far too loud; loud chords no longer distort; the
  music is no longer too quiet next to effects and speech.
- Turtles in Time: the picture is the board's real 304 pixels wide and the
  backgrounds line up; sprites overlap in the right order (the turtle in the
  intro circle is no longer hidden); zoomed sprites such as the "Evening
  News" letters are exact; shadows only darken the background, at the right
  strength; the news-scene fade and the prehistoric night darkening work,
  with bright light beams; Krang's legs and other big sprites use the right
  pieces; sampled sounds and voices were too quiet next to the music.
- The Simpsons: sprites were 16 pixels too high; big sprites (the jewellery
  store door) had wrong pieces; enemies blinked or showed where they
  shouldn't; shadows (cage bars, bosses, Krusty) were wrong and the title
  and score screens were darkened by mistake; sprites line up during the
  final boss's screen shake; sampled sounds were too quiet next to the music.
- Music chip (all three games): noise level and fade fixed, and loud notes
  no longer distort.
- New CHANGELOG.txt lists the changes in every version.

RetroPixels 2.6.2  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- TMNT: fire no longer shows in front of the doors in the burning
  building. Sprites now stack in the right order, hidden duplicate sprites
  (like an extra turtle in the demo) are gone, the backgrounds sit 6 pixels
  further right where they belong, and shadows are a little lighter. The
  picture now matches the arcade board's own layering chip.

RetroPixels 2.6.1  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- Recording on every system now captures only the game picture. NES, SNES,
  Game Boy, GBA, N64, Genesis, Master System, Atari and arcade recordings
  no longer include black bars, buttons, messages or the rest of the
  window, and are saved at a sharp, even size. (GameCube / Wii already
  recorded only the game since 2.6.0.)
- TMNT: the game's own ROM self-test now passes, speech sounds cleaner,
  and the service buttons work for players 2 to 4.
- The Simpsons: sprites were drawn too far to the right of the backgrounds
  (character select, intro, bonus stage); they now line up. Ground shadows
  draw as full ovals instead of thin slivers.

RetroPixels 2.6.0  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- Arcade: TMNT, Turtles in Time (TMNT2) and The Simpsons now boot and play
  with correct graphics and sound. Fixed: TMNT's graphics and sprite layout,
  sound effects and speech; TMNT2's chip list, settings memory, interrupts
  and sprites; The Simpsons' processor and settings memory. Sets without
  their .nv file set themselves up on first start.
- GameCube / Wii recording rebuilt: Dolphin now saves the game's own frames
  and sound while you play, and RetroPixels turns them into an .mp4 when the
  game ends. The video is smooth and shows only the game: no mouse cursor, no
  other windows, no black bars. "Saving recording…" shows while it's made.
  With automatic recording off, the Record button records the next game.

RetroPixels 2.5.2  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- GameCube / Wii recording: black bars are now always trimmed from the
  finished video (before, only with Aspect ratio on "Auto"). If ffmpeg isn't
  installed, the first game you start downloads it and recording starts
  when it's ready, instead of not recording.

RetroPixels 2.5.1  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- GameCube / Wii: a volume slider and mute button in the bar above the game
  while it plays, and under "Sound" in the GameCube / Wii settings. Windows
  remembers Dolphin's volume, so it may also apply if you open Dolphin by
  itself.
- Arcade: ROM sets are recognised even when the zip has a different name
  (for example "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (World).zip" or "tmnt (1).zip"),
  by the chip files inside. If a zip still isn't recognised, the message now
  lists the files inside it.

RetroPixels 2.5.0  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- Arcade: Konami's Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Turtles in Time (TMNT2) and
  The Simpsons, with up to four players. New from-scratch chips: Konami-2
  processor, YM2151, K052109, K051960, K007232, uPD7759, K053260, K053245 /
  K053247, K053251 and the 93C46 EEPROM.
- Arcade sets whose chip files are named differently are matched by size.
- GameCube / Wii: "Add games folder…" now opens the folder picker in front of
  RetroPixels (it could open hidden), looks deeper inside the folder, and says
  how many games it found.

RetroPixels 2.4.0  (2026-10-14)
-------------------------------
- New Arcade system, written from scratch: Puck Man / Pac-Man, Galaxian,
  Space Invaders and Donkey Kong. Load the game's .zip ROM set. Includes
  RetroPixels' own Intel 8080 and 8035 processors, and a "Machine settings"
  panel for each game's DIP switches.

RetroPixels 2.3.0  (2026-10-13)
-------------------------------
- Super Nintendo: DSP-3, DSP-4, ST010, ST011 and ST018 games now play. These
  chips need their built-in program, which RetroPixels can't include: the
  first time, it asks you to choose your own dump of it (dsp3.rom, dsp4.rom,
  st010.rom, st011.rom or st018.rom) and remembers it.
- Originality check: every file was reviewed and the parts that followed other
  emulators too closely were rewritten in RetroPixels' own way (DSP-1, Cx4,
  S-DD1 and SPC7110 chips, SNES sound filter, 68000 divide timing, Atari 2600
  cartridge detection and DPC music, Game Boy Color colours, Genesis and
  Master System colours, GBA BIOS ArcTan, N64 6105 boot check and Controller
  Pak checksum, the 7-Zip ARM64 filter).
- Things you may notice: Konami VRC7 games (Lagrange Point) use RetroPixels'
  own instrument sounds; Game Boy Color colours look slightly different;
  DSP-1 games (Pilotwings, Super Mario Kart) compute 3D maths slightly
  differently from the real chip.

RetroPixels 2.2.0  (2026-10-13)
-------------------------------
- Super Nintendo: games with cartridge chips now play: Super FX (Star Fox,
  Yoshi's Island), SA-1 (Super Mario RPG, Kirby Super Star), DSP-1 (Super
  Mario Kart, Pilotwings), DSP-2, Cx4 (Mega Man X2 / X3), S-DD1 (Star Ocean),
  SPC7110, OBC1 and S-RTC.
- NES: about 120 cartridge types (up from 18) and the Famicom sound chips
  (VRC6, VRC7, Namco 163, Sunsoft 5B, MMC5); many accuracy fixes.
- Game Boy / Color: more cartridge chips (MBC6, MBC7 tilt, HuC3, TAMA5, rumble
  and others) and accuracy fixes (passes the blargg, mooneye and acid2 tests).
- Game Boy Advance: sensor cartridges (Boktai sun sensor, WarioWare: Twisted!
  gyro, Yoshi Topsy-Turvy tilt, rumble) and BIOS call fixes.
- Genesis: EEPROM saves, Realtec mapper, J-Cart 4-player games, automatic
  3-button pad mode.
- New: Sega Master System / Mark III.
- Atari 2600: nearly every bank-switching scheme, DPC+ and the Supercharger.
  Atari 7800: more cartridge types and POKEY sound.
- The version number shows in the app.

RetroPixels 2.1.0  (2026-10-13)
-------------------------------
- Nintendo 64 runs on RetroPixels' own emulator inside RetroPixels.exe
  (EmulatorJS removed; no internet needed).
- "Download Dolphin" button in the GameCube tab.
- GameCube / Wii games play inside the RetroPixels window; Stop game closes
  Dolphin completely.

Before 2.1.0  (2026-10-13)
--------------------------
- RetroConsole became RetroPixels. Settings, save states, recordings and save
  backups move over from RetroConsole automatically the first time it starts.
- Recordings follow the game's own volume.
