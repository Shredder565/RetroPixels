RetroPixels 2.2.0
Released 2026-10-13

What's new in 2.2.0
- Super Nintendo: games with cartridge chips now play: Super FX (Star Fox,
  Yoshi's Island), SA-1 (Super Mario RPG, Kirby Super Star), DSP-1 (Super
  Mario Kart, Pilotwings), DSP-2, Cx4 (Mega Man X2 / X3), S-DD1 (Star Ocean),
  SPC7110, OBC1 and S-RTC.
- NES: about 120 cartridge types (up from 18) and the Famicom sound chips
  (VRC6, VRC7, Namco 163, Sunsoft 5B, MMC5); many accuracy fixes.
- Game Boy / Color: more cartridge chips (MBC6, MBC7 tilt, HuC3, TAMA5, rumble
  and others) and accuracy fixes (passes the blargg, mooneye and acid2 tests).
- Game Boy Advance: sensor cartridges (Boktai sun sensor, WarioWare: Twisted!
  gyro, Yoshi Topsy-Turvy tilt, rumble) and BIOS call fixes.
- Genesis: EEPROM saves, Realtec mapper, J-Cart 4-player games, automatic
  3-button pad mode.
- New: Sega Master System / Mark III.
- Atari 2600: nearly every bank-switching scheme, DPC+ and the Supercharger.
  Atari 7800: more cartridge types and POKEY sound.
- The version number shows in the app.

2.1.0
- Nintendo 64 runs on RetroPixels' own emulator inside RetroPixels.exe
  (EmulatorJS removed; no internet needed).
- "Download Dolphin" button in the GameCube tab.

  Dolphin completely.
